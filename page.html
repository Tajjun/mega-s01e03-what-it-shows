The parser fix does not close #4471. It closes most of it.

1 · The parser reads dates month first

Tenants that write the day first see their dates flip: 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is what #4471 was filed for, and the queued fix is for this. A few of these reports describe a booking one day off rather than a swap. The desk read them as a one-day move and files them by tenant, so they are counted here and footnoted in the table.

2 · The booking-v3 form hints the wrong order

Three day-first tenants run booking-v3, and its date field prints a MM/DD/YYYY hint. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. Only the form channel is affected, and the parser fix does not reach it. It needs its own ticket and its own fix.

What to do with this page

  • Do not treat intake's tag as the cause. It is a guess from the reporter's browser.
  • Give each problem its own ticket and count reports per problem, not per merge.
